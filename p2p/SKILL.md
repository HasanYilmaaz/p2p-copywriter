## Core Philosophy

P2P is **direct response copywriting**: every piece of copy must drive a measurable action. Not brand awareness, not creativity for its own sake.

**The 3 pillars:**
1. **Simplicity** -- Write for a 10-year-old. If the reader has to think about the message, it fails.
2. **Proven frameworks** -- Follow structures that already work. Originality adds unknown variables, and unknown variables are risk.
3. **Deep problem understanding** -- Describe the reader's problem better than they can. Whoever names the problem owns it.

**Ingredients before recipe.** The quality of copy is decided by the research (problems, dualities, angles, mechanism), not by the structure it is poured into. Thin ingredients mean research first.

**The Theory of Dualities.** Duality creates conflict, conflict creates movement, movement breeds decision. Every strong piece of copy carries a duality and leaves the conflict open for the reader to resolve by acting.

**The opening has the most leverage.** Most readers and viewers are lost in the first seconds. Write several openings and choose; a better opening over the same body sends far more people to the call to action.

## Quick Reference: 5 Basic Elements

Gather these before writing anything:
1. **Target Customer** -- demographics plus behavior, specific enough that the reader says "that's me"
2. **Desired Outcome** -- the specific result in their head ("lose 3 kg of belly fat", not "lose weight")
3. **Benefit** -- what makes it better (time, ease: "in under 45 days")
4. **Pain Point** -- the biggest obstacle or objection, handled in advance ("without having to...")
5. **Signature Solution** -- a unique name that avoids the category word ("protocol", not "diet")

## Quick Reference: The 6-Step Formula

1. **Verify** -- start with something they have experienced (gets inside the self-sealing logic bubble)
2. **Validate the internal problem** -- how it makes them feel
3. **Pivot to the philosophical problem** -- introduce the duality
4. **Explanation** -- the logical reason why (logic justifies the emotional decision)
5. **Benefit** -- make the next step irresistible
6. **Call to Action** -- tell them exactly what to do

In emails and ads the call to action sells the click, not the product; the page or video does the selling.

## Quick Reference: 3 Layers of Problems

External (what happens day to day) --> Internal (how it makes them feel) --> Philosophical (the conflict or violated natural order behind it) --> Real desire. Copy leads with the internal problem for recognition, then pivots to the philosophical problem for awareness.
