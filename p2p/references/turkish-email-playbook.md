# Turkish Email Playbook (House Style From a Sent Archive)

Operating instructions for planning and writing Turkish emails for online education: free events and launches, 21-day paid "kamp" cohorts, weekly member broadcasts, short offers, onboarding. Read it with `email-machine.md` (system rules), `email-campaign-blueprints.md` (P2P sequences), `copy-formulas.md` (6-step, PWAS), `proven-angles.md`, `patron-notes.md` section 5, `turkish-subject-lines.md` (subject patterns, formatting, do-not-copy list) and `turkish-email-swipe.md` (full specimens).

## 1. What this file is and how to use it

**Evidence.** A Turkish online-education company's sent-email archive: about 1,930 unique bodies, 2022 to 2026, read in eight chronological parts. The archive holds **no open, click or revenue data**. The only popularity signal is the **reuse count** (how many times a body was sent again, up to x163 for a reminder template). Reuse shows what the team kept using, mostly because it was operationally convenient. Nothing here is "proven to perform"; say "the house uses" and never "this converts".

**How to use it.**
1. Plan the calendar with section 2 (architectures) and section 6 (procedure).
2. For each slot pick a type from section 3 and write from its skeleton.
3. Take openings, bridges, CTAs and closings from section 4; default to the current style in section 5.
4. Model the matching specimen in `turkish-email-swipe.md`. Never paste one.
5. Subject lines: `turkish-subject-lines.md`.

**Anonymisation.** `[Kurucu]` founder, `[Ekip Üyesi]` staff, `[Marka]`, `[Program]`, `[Etkinlik]`, `[Öğrenci]`, `{{isim}}`, `[fiyat]`. Replace with the user's real facts; ask when a fact is missing.

**Where the archive and P2P disagree, P2P wins.** The archive often sends 3 to 5 emails a day to one segment, uses banners, and pushes offers to people who never saw them. `email-machine.md` section 8 stays in force: urgency only to people who know the offer, buyers exit at once, scarcity must be true, and every campaign ends back in value emails.

**Do not copy.** The archive also contains income guarantees, unsourced statistics, system-alert style subjects, simulated forwards, reopened deadlines and fear framing. The full list is in `turkish-subject-lines.md`; none of it belongs in new copy.

## 2. Campaign architectures

T = event start. Times are the house defaults (evening broadcast at 20.30; older years 21.00). "Team" = `biz` voice, "Founder" = `ben` voice.

### (a) Free event / launch (1 to 3 evenings) and the sale after it

| When | Email | Voice | Purpose | CTA target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-10 to T-4, one a day | Invite ladder: a new angle each send (maxim, "wrong question", economy scene, story) | Founder or team | Registration | Registration page |
| Same weeks | Founder content mail (video) that seeds the theme | Founder | Warm-up | Video |
| Right after registration, then T-5, T-2, T-1 | Community-join push (flow e) | Team | Move registrants into the reminder channel | WhatsApp community |
| T-4 to T-1, daily | Belief-breaking email, one version per declared avatar (business owner / employee / beginner) | Team, sen | Replace one old belief; build attendance | Community join only |
| T-1 evening | Event-eve story email | Team or founder | Lock in attendance | No link: "Yarın 20:30'a alarmını kur." |
| T day, morning | Day-of mail (story, FAQ or agenda angle) | Founder | Attendance | Join/registration link |
| T-3h, T-15min | Reminder duo | Ops, siz | Get into the room | Live link |
| T | "Başladık" | Ops | Click into the room now | Live link |
| During broadcast (optional) | One short "devam ediyor" mail; older years sent 2 to 6 one-paragraph mails plus an offer link during the pitch | Ops | Stragglers | Live link |
| Days 2 and 3 | Morning: recap + gifts for attendees + tonight's teaser; evening: mails split by avatar or declared goal; a separate version for "came day 1, missed day 2" | Team | Keep the series alive | Gifts page / live link |
| T+1 | Replay or "panelde" notice with gifts, then the offer | Team | Non-attendees catch up; open the sale | Replay, then appointment |
| T+1 to T+6 | Sales by appointment: short anticipated-regret mail, founder story, proof, two-groups email, objection email | Founder and team alternate | Book a call | Appointment form |
| Last 48h / 24h / last day | Quota or deadline countdown, then close | Founder | Decide | Appointment form |
| After purchase | Orientation invite x3 + "Başladık"; buyer leaves the sales flow | Team | Start the cohort | Orientation link |
| Cohort day 1 (optional) | Non-buyers invited to watch day 1 live as the final push (2 to 4 story mails, last one "Ve Final...") | Team | Last chance | Live link |

Older variant: one 70-minute evening webinar repeated weekly or monthly, a gifts-plus-offer mail minutes after it, a long cart letter, and an "encore" invite for no-shows.

### (b) 21-day paid cohort lifecycle

| Phase | Emails | Voice | Purpose | CTA target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Purchase to start | Welcome ("Sıkı tutunun. Başlıyoruz."), numbered access steps (panel, group, community), orientation invite, reminder duo, "Başladık", replay | Founder welcome, team logistics | Activation | Panel, groups, orientation |
| Prep week | Prep-lesson uploads; 2 to 3 story-opened mails that push "finish prep + register for day 1"; one task mail | Team, siz | Arrive prepared | Lesson / registration |
| Day 1 | "Başlıyoruz" day-of, reminder duo, "Başladık"; next morning replay + the promised gift; class or track assignment | Founder + ops | Start | Live link, panel |
| Days 2 to 6 | Founder teaser-video mails, homework nudge in coach voice, "kafan mı karıştı" help mail, one student story to non-starters | Founder / coach | Daily motion | Group video, panel |
| End of week 1 | Live block: 3 invites in different voices over 48h, day-of, reminders, replay ("Müjde! Panele Yüklendi!") | Team, founder, coach | Attendance | Registration, replay |
| Week 2 | Founder live ("Ben [Kurucu], buluşmamız lazım"), make-up invite for no-shows, replay that pre-sells the graduation seminar | Founder | Attendance + anticipation | Registration, replay |
| Catch-up, any time | "Geride kaldın" re-engagement, two-step restart mail, a "reward" extra broadcast for consistent students | Team / coach | Re-engage | Panel, form |
| T-5 to T-1 of the "mezuniyet semineri" | Announce ("Kamp'ın En Büyük Günü!"), teaser video mails, day-before mail, registration chase to non-registrants (up to 4 angles on T-1) | Founder | Fill the sales event | Seminar registration |
| Seminar day | Objection/SSS mail, sunk-effort mail ("21 gün emek verdin…"), reminder duo, "Başladık" | Founder + ops | Live attendance | Live link |
| After the seminar | Gifts + offer (offer first, gifts second); then an access or deadline countdown used as the second sales window, each mail with one student result | Founder | Upsell by appointment | Appointment form, gift links |
| Completion | "21 Gün Bitti…" next-step mail, alumni group invite, survey ("Sadece 30 saniye") | Team | Close the loop | Form, group |

The seminar is the sale; emails only fill the room and follow up. The company reran this skeleton monthly with the same bodies, which is why reuse counts are highest here.

### (c) Weekly member live broadcast micro-cycle

| When | Email | Purpose | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Same day (or T-1) | Agenda mail, subject "Konu (Bu Akşam 20.30)" | Register for tonight's Zoom | `➡️ [Buradan Kaydol]` top and bottom |
| T | `{{isim}} Başladık!` | Enter now | `[Buraya]` inline link |
| T+1 (optional) | Replay / "panelde" | Watch the recording | Panel |

Older variant, six mails: designed card the day before, "bu akşam" invite, "Hatırlatma - Son 3 Saat", "Son 15 Dakika {{isim}}", "Başladık", "Panelinize Yüklendi" plus an occasional feedback form. Default to the current two or three.

### (d) Short coupon / discount push (24 to 72 hours)

| When | Email | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Open | Offer mail | Reason-why for the discount, mechanism in numbered steps, what is included, exact end time |
| Middle (about 12h left) | Reminder with one new argument | Proof number or one objection |
| Last 6h / 3h | Short mail with one new idea | Price reframed against a daily expense, or a risk-reversal line |
| Last 2h / 60 min | Close | Three-step how-to, exact deadline in "Not:" |

Appointment variant, three mails: "open this before midnight" → "indirim bahane, asıl haber içeride" → last-hours mail. Seat-countdown variant, three mails with the true number of seats left. Send only to people who know the offer, and only when the code, price change or seat limit is real and will not quietly return.

### (e) Community join / "bir adım eksik" push

| When | Framing | Key line |
|---|---|---|
| Minutes after registration | Step missing | "Kaydın tamamlandı. Ama bir adım eksik kaldı." |
| +1 day | Spam risk | "Maillerin en önemli kısımları bazen spam kutusuna düşüyor." |
| T-2 | Loss list | "📢 Kaçıracakların: …" |
| T-1 | Last step | Subject pattern "[Program] Eğitimi Yarın Başlıyor (Son Adım)"; body ends "2–3–4 Nisan. Saat 20:30. Tekrar yok." (dates as real) |

Every version carries the reassurance block (nobody can post, phone number hidden, only critical notices) and a QR image. Do not write "kaydın tamamlanmadı" when the registration is in fact complete; say that one step is missing. Stop the push for anyone who joined.

### (f) Crisis, bad news, postponement

| Step | Email | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stop / news notice, founder-signed. The news sits in the first line ("Merhaba {{isim}}, [platform] kapatıldı…") | Facts, reassurance, what happens next ("Bizden haber bekleyin…"). No selling |
| 2 | Practical help or interim plan (numbered) | Still no offer |
| 3 | New date or solution: "🗓️ yeni tarih", "Not: Yeniden kayıt oluşturmanıza gerek yoktur. Transferiniz otomatik gerçekleştirilecektir." | One action |
| 4 | Resume mail that states the doubt aloud ("Bugün kendi kendime 'Şimdi sırası mı?' diye düşündüm.") then the agenda | Honest reason |
| 5 | First broadcast in the new setup, with access instructions (invitations are going out, check the spam folder) | Back to flow (c) |

Small errors get a two-line fix: "Düzeltme: … Güncel giriş linki". The archive's discounts tied to a national disaster are a flagged pattern; do not attach offers to a crisis.

### (g) Onboarding mini-camp for buyers (3 to 5 days)

| When | Email | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| T-3 to T-1 | Announce + orientation mails ("Harika, ama ben hangisinden başlayacağım?" → "Endişelenme.") | Set expectations |
| Each session day | 2 or 3 invites, each a different angle on the same agenda, then "Başladık" | Attendance |
| Next morning | Replay + checklists + homework ("🎯 Ödevlerin:") | Application |
| Mid-camp | Homework with a prize, status form ("Hangi aşamadasın?"), reminders by segment | Gamification, segmentation |
| Last day | Progress recap in past-tense bullets → "Ve şimdi… sıra son adıma geldi!" | Finish |
| T+1 after the end | "Mini Kamp Bitti. Sırada Ne Var?" with all replays | Hand-off to weekly broadcasts |

## 3. Email type catalogue

Each entry: job · when · length · skeleton · voice and CTA · P2P structure. Swipe numbers refer to `turkish-email-swipe.md`.

**1. Reminder trio ("Son 3 Saat / Son 15 Dakika / Başladık").** Job: get registered people into the room. T-3h, T-15min, T. 25 to 70 words. Skeleton: `Merhaba {{isim}},` → one sentence with time, event title and one CAPS token → one link sentence → "Sevgiler." The newer "Başladık" adds one line on what is on screen "şu an". Ops voice. No persuasion and no P2P structure; the subject does the work. Swipe 2, 3.

**2. Agenda mail ("Bu Akşam").** Job: register members for tonight. Day-of. 70 to 130 words. Skeleton: greeting → pain question in the reader's words or a contrarian claim → one reframe → CTA → "Bu akşam 20.30'da canlı yayında;" → 3 bullets → one line naming who it is for → same CTA. Team voice. P2P: compressed 6-step (verify → problem → pivot → benefit bullets → CTA). Swipe 1.

**3. Lesson-night invite with a problem scene.** Job: sell tonight's session to people who already paid. 70 to 130 words. Skeleton: "Gerçek senaryo:" scene → consequence → who does it right → "Bu akşam canlı yayında:" 3 bullets → benefit-named button → closing aphorism. P2P: verify → problem → explanation → benefit → CTA. Swipe 4.

**4. Multi-angle session invite.** Job: 2 or 3 sends for one session without repeating. 110 to 170 words. Skeleton: contrast scene (amatör / profesyonel) or a named micro-fear → verdict line → session name → "Bu Akşam Neleri Çözeceğiz?" with `emoji + Etiket: soru` bullets → aphorism → CTA twice → "Bekliyoruz." P2P: the amateur/professional contrast is the house form of the philosophical step. Swipe 5.

**5. "3 soru maddesi" invitation.** Job: registration for a broadcast. T-1 or day-of. 80 to 180 words. Skeleton: hook question → why this topic matters → CTA → exactly 3 question bullets ("Nasıl…?", "Hangi…?", "Neden…?") → "Tüm detayları canlı yayında konuşuyoruz…" → same CTA. Team voice. P2P: "the email sells the click"; bullets are open loops and answers stay in the broadcast. Swipe 9, 10.

**6. Story-opened invitation.** Job: warm a list toward an event or a start date. 120 to 350 words. Skeleton: surprising fact or parable (no greeting) → turn ("Ama…") → lesson in one line → bridge with the name ("Hazırlığın önemi bu kadar büyük {{isim}}.") → what/when → CTA → optional 3 bullets → closing line that returns to the story image → CTA. P2P: proven angle (borrowed story) carrying a compressed 6-step. Check every historical fact. Swipe 7, 8.

**7. Event-eve story email.** Job: lock in attendance the night before. 230 to 300 words, no CTA link. Skeleton: famous-company or founder story → bridge "Sen kendi şirketinin [hikâye karakteri]sin" → what you leave with tomorrow → "everything else can wait" → "Yarın 20:30'a alarmını kur." → "Mutlaka bilgisayardan katılmaya çalış." Example bridge: "Ray Kroc, onlardan daha lezzetli bir hamburger yapmadı. Sadece o dükkanın operasyonunu, satışını ve pazarlamasını bir 'sisteme' bağladı. … Hamburger pişirmekten sistemi göremiyorsun."

**8. Belief-breaking avatar email.** Job: before the event, swap one old belief for the new opportunity. T-4 to T-1, one version per avatar. 180 to 280 words, one sentence per line, no exclamation marks. Skeleton: the reader's daily reality → hidden cost → "Çünkü sana yıllarca … öğretildi" (blame the old rule) → "Fakat bu 1990'ların kuralıydı." → one analogy carried to the end → new role → dates → one imperative → community link. P2P: full 6-step; sells only the next step. Swipe 6.

**9. Short curiosity bridge to a video (founder teaser).** Job: one click to a video in the group or on YouTube. 40 to 110 words. Skeleton: concrete scene or confession → 3 to 6 one-line beats → "Neden mi?" → explicit withholding ("Detayları burada anlatmayacağım.") → one link → optional "Not:" on where the video lives. Founder voice. P2P: the purest "email sells the click". Swipe 12 to 15.

**10. Different-voice resend.** Job: a second or third send for the same event that reads as a new email. See section 4. Forms: founder `ben` teaser + team `biz` logistics on the same day; coach in first person; a team member introducing themselves ("Ben [Ekip Üyesi]. Bu defa iyi haberleri verme fırsatı benim oldu."); the "three news" digest ("O zaman size 3 güzel haberim var." → replay, progress form, next broadcast, one CTA per item). P2P: `patron-notes.md` multiple voices.

**11. Founder nudge.** Job: personal push before a key broadcast, or to engaged people who have not registered. Day-of or T-1. 80 to 200 words. Skeleton A (confession): unexpected admission → why the reader's result matters to the founder → time → one CTA → human close ("Lütfen kendine bir iyilik yap ve yayına gel."). Skeleton B (registration chase): registration status → name the excuse ("Daha eğitimleri bitirmedim, hazır hissedince izlerim…") → flip it ("Mükemmel zaman diye bir şey yoktur.") → imperative → link. Send B only to a segment that is filtered as not registered, and do not claim a personal manual check that did not happen. Swipe 16.

**12. Community-join push.** See flow (e). 40 to 90 words. CTA "🟢 [WhatsApp Topluluğuna Katılmak İçin Tıkla]". Swipe 20.

**13. Re-engagement ("geride kaldın").** Job: pull lagging buyers back. 60 to 220 words. Skeleton: validate the purchase or effort → name the trap ("Erteleme Çukuru") → two groups → "hâlâ zaman var" → what the next live gives → CTA. Caring lines: "Gözlerimiz seni aradı ama sanırım bir aksilik oldu. / Hiç sorun değil, çünkü tren henüz kaçmadı."; "Biraz hızlanırsan arayı kapatabilirsin… / Hâlâ yetişebilirsin."; restart in two numbered steps with "Tek şart başlamak." P2P: verify → internal problem → duality → CTA. No shaming. Swipe 18.

**14. Replay / "panelde" notice.** Job: get the recording watched, bridge to the next event. T+1 morning. 60 to 180 words. Skeleton: session name → one-line maxim → "Eğer yayını kaçırdıysan, şu stratejileri de kaçırdın demektir:" → 3 to 4 bullets → "Neyse ki telafisi var." → link → optional teaser or registration for the next live. Fast method: reuse the invitation, change the tense (cevaplıyor → cevapladı) and the CTA. Swipe 21.

**15. Promise-kept bonus / gift delivery.** Job: deliver and make the gift feel earned. 60 to 250 words. Skeleton: "Söz vermiştik. / Sözümüzü tuttuk." → one analogy that sells the bonus → link. After a sales event the house puts the offer first and the gifts second, with the reframe: "Hediyeler, ücretsiz olduğu için sanki içerisinde gerçekten emek verilmiş ve masraf edilmiş bir şey yok gibi davranırız." Swipe 19.

**16. Objection / SSS email.** Job: remove reasons not to attend or not to book. Seminar day or sales window. 150 to 300 words. Skeleton: "son durumları kontrol ederken bir yanlışlık fark ettim" → the reader's objections in their own words, each answered "Hayır, …" → link → 3 teaser bullets → link. Sales-phase forms: "öğrenme modundan çıkıp aksiyon moduna geçemiyoruz", "Kolay değil ama basit." P2P: `patron-notes.md` (warm and stuck: argue inside the email). Swipe 22.

**17. Social-proof story.** Job: let a student result argue. 80 to 200 words. Skeleton: one-line before/after per student, each with its own link → "Onlar izlemeyi bıraktı ve ilerlemeye karar verdi." → two-futures line → CTA to a fit call → "Not: Bu, herkese göre değil." Use only real, attributable results. Swipe 24.

**18. Sales email to an appointment.** Job: book a call with the team; the house never links a checkout for high-ticket offers. 90 to 350 words. Forms: founder chat ("Biraz dertleşelim…"), two-path offer (each path with its own CTA), offer stack of three bullets + proof video + two-option close. Honest note that also qualifies: "Not: Görüşmenin ardından, karşılıklı uygunsa ekibimiz içeriye girişini onaylayacak." P2P: 6-step or PWAS; only to people who saw the offer. Swipe 25.

**19. Quota / deadline countdown.** Job: move deciders. Three clocks exist: event clock (N gün kaldı → yarın → bu akşam → başladık), access clock after the event (Son 2 Gün → Yarın → Son Saatler), quota clock (percentage filled → seats left). Rule from the best specimens: each countdown mail carries one new proof item or idea, not only the timer. 60 to 200 words. P2P: countdown layer in `email-machine.md`.

**20. Close (last day).** Model: the short anticipated-regret mail. Verify by quoting what late readers write ("'Gerçekten mi? Hâlâ zamanım var zannediyordum.'") → excuse the reader ("Belki o an telefon çaldı. / Belki internet bağlantısı koptu.") → relief ("Sebep her ne olursa olsun hâlâ vaktin var.") → one-sentence offer → one CTA → qualifying "Not:". After the stated close, send nothing that reopens it. Coupon version: swipe 26.

**21. Onboarding welcome.** Job: prevent buyer's remorse, get the orientation attended. 120 to 200 words. Skeleton: "Sıkı tutunun. Başlıyoruz." → congratulation → "we do not disappear after payment" line → orientation time → 3 question bullets → "akıllıca bir yatırım yaptınız; şimdi en yüksek kârı almak için…" Swipe 23.

**22. Smaller types.** Homework/gamification (recap line → "Artık tribünden izlemeyi bırakıp, oyuna dahil olma vakti." → numbered tasks → prize → form link twice; follow-ups: winners, deadline). Founder content mail (60 to 150 words, 3 bullets, link twice; swipe 17). Product or topic broadcast in PWAS (problem → wish → answer → 3 benefits → one CTA; swipe 11). Appointment reminder (logistics checklist + "hayal ettiğiniz hayatla aranızda sadece bir randevu kaldı"). Survey (3 to 5 lines, button "(Sadece 30 saniye)"). Correction and postponement notices (flow f). Mini-camp daily mail (status line → emoji agenda → CTA → homework → yesterday's replay).

## 4. Reusable copy moves

**Openings**
- Present-tense scene: "Haberleri açıyorsun, ekonomi gündemde." / "Kurallara eksiksiz uydun." / "Gerçek senaryo:"
- Place and motion (founder): "Şu an [Ülke]'de bir feribottayım." / "Bugün sabahın köründe havalimanındaydım."
- Maxim: "Gemiler limanda güvende olur. / Ama gemi limanda durmak için yapılmamıştır."
- Direct: "Direkt konuya giriyorum {{isim}}." / "Biraz dertleşelim…" / "Dürüst olalım;"
- Promise kept: "Söz verdim ve sözümü tutmaya geldim."
- Reader's inner voice in quotes: "'Hesabım yeni, yapamam.'" / "Bu cümleyi aklından geçirmeyen kaldı mı?"
- Callback triple: "… söylemiştik. 'Hazır olun.' demiştik. O gün geldi."
- Absurd question set: three impossible contests → "Cevap hep aynı."

**Bridges (each on its own line)**
"Neden mi?" · "Nasıl mı?" · "Nereden mi biliyorum?" · "Peki bunu size neden anlatıyorum?" · "Hayat da böyle." (analogy → lesson) · "Ama…" · "Peki ya sen?" · "Sonuç mu?" · "Bitti mi? Bitmedi." · "Burada bitmedi." · "Gerçek şu:" · "Asıl soru şu:" · "İşte tam bu noktada … devreye giriyor." · "Güzel haber şu ki;" · "Aramızda kalsın;" · "Unutma."

**Withholding lines (sell the click)**
"Detayları burada anlatmayacağım." · "Tek önemli soru var. O da burada 👇🏻" · "Hepsinin cevabı bu videoda." · "Bu maile yazsaydık sığmazdı."

**CTA lines and button texts**
- Register: `➡️ [Buradan Kaydol]` (current default) · older polite form "Buradan Kaydolabilirsiniz" · "Buraya Tıklayarak Yerini Ayır".
- Watch: "▶️ [Buradan İzle]" · "Yayın Kaydını Buradan İzle" · with time cost: "Buradan İzleyebilirsiniz (4 dakika)".
- Live: "[Buraya] tıklayarak yayına bağlanabilirsiniz."
- Sales: "Buraya Tıkla ve Randevu Al" · "💎 Uzman Ekipten Randevu Al" · first person: "Uzman Ekipten Bilgi Almak İstiyorum".
- Benefit-named: "👉 Satış Kapama Yayınına Kaydol" · "👉 Katıl, birlikte pratiğe geçelim!"
- Date or time in the button: "Buradan Kaydol (8 Ocak)" · "[Buradan Kaydol] (Bu akşam TR 20.30)".
- Community: "Henüz katılmadıysan WhatsApp Topluluğuna Katıl".
- Placement: the same CTA text 2 or 3 times (after the first 4 to 8 lines, after the bullets, at the end).

**Closings**
"Karar senin." · "Görüşmek üzere." · "Bekliyoruz." · "Kağıdını kalemini hazırla." · "Çayını kahveni al, not defterini hazırla." · "Yarın 20:30'a alarmını kur." · "Mutlaka bilgisayardan katılmaya çalış."

**PS ("Not:", never "P.S.")**
- Qualifier: "Not: Bu, herkese göre değil. Bu, sadece izlemeyi bırakıp aksiyona geçmek için gerçekten karar verenler için."
- Objection remover: "Not: Ödevleri yapmakta geride kalanlar da bu seminere katılarak bütün avantajlarından faydalanabilir."
- Logistics: "Not: Eğitimden tam verim alabilmen için bilgisayardan katılman önerilir."
- Cadence, honest: "Not: Yayına kaydolursanız davet mailleri yerine yayına az zaman kala hatırlatma mailleri alacaksınız."
- Access: "Not: Eğer özel gruba katılmadıysan bu videoyu göremezsin. [Buraya] tıklayarak katılabilirsin."
- Exact deadline: "Not: [kod] bu gece 23:59'a kadar aktif kalacak."
- Aphorism instead of a statistic: "Karın kasların olunca spora başlamazsın. Spora başlayınca karın kasların olur."

**Two groups / two futures**
- "Ya hikayeleri okumaya devam edersin ya da kendi hikayeni yazmaya başlarsın."
- "Büyük değişim dönemleri hep iki grup ortaya çıkarır: - Geride kalanlar. - Yeni dönemi anlayanlar."
- "Şu an önünde iki seçenek var: - Hâlâ 'doğru zamanı' bekleyip 6 ay sonra bugünkü yerinde saymaya devam etmek. - Bir karar verip randevunu almak…"
- "Yeni yıla 'keşke' diyerek değil, 'iyi ki' diyerek başla."
- "Yayını kaçırırsan, tek başına öğrenmeye çalışırsın. / Ama katılırsan, hazır protokollerle hızlanırsın."
- Role pairs: seçilen/seçen, operatör/mimar, amatör/profesyonel, izleyen/kuran, tribün/oyun.
- Ladder: "Sıradan bir girişimci problemi görür. / İyi bir girişimci çözümü bulur. / Kıskanılan bir girişimci ise kimsenin görmediği fırsatı yaratır."

**Blame-lifting lines**
"Bunun sebebi cesaretsizlik veya isteksizlik değil." · "Sorun sizde değil, yöntemlerinizde." · "Çünkü sorun tembellik değil." · "Harekete geçen insanlar senden daha azimli ya da daha yetenekli değil. Sadece ellerinde somut bir yapı var." · "Bu karar rasyoneldi. Sistem eksikti." · "Sorun 'bilgi eksikliği' değil. / Sorun 'bilgi zehirlenmesi'." · "Kimse yeteneksiz ya da zayıf değildir. / Kullandığı yöntemler yüzünden başarıya ulaşamazlar." The enemy is an old rule or a habit ("Bu eski dünyanın kuralıydı.", "Erteleme Çukuru", "Analiz Felci"), never the reader and never a named competitor.

**Aphorisms and analogies (one per email, carried to the last line)**
"Ertelemek, 'hayır' demenin en kaçak yoludur." · "Değeri olmayan bir teklifin fiyatı, ne kadar düşük tutulursa tutulsun hep pahalıdır." · "Geç kalmadınız ama her ertelediğiniz adımda gecikiyorsunuz." · "Tavaya yumurta kırarsan yumurta yersin." Analogy bank: 99 ve 100 derece su; boş viteste gaza basmak; doktor ve hastane; temeli olmayan bina; maraton antrenmanı; pusula ve harita; filmi ortasından izlemek; altına hücumda kürek satan.

**Social proof phrasing**
One-line before/after ("Yoğun nöbetler arasında çalışan bir hemşireydi. … Şu an hem Türkiye'de hem de Avrupa'da müşterilere iş yapıyor.") · timed result ("mağazasını açalı daha 9 gün olmasına rağmen ilk siparişini aldı") · equaliser ("Onlar da senin gibi başladılar.") · hand-off ("sözü kursiyerlerime bırakıyorum:" + screenshots). Use real, attributable results; never typical-income implications.

**Urgency that stays honest** (each line is usable only if the constraint named is real)
- No replay, if truly none: "Bu seminerin tekrarı yok. Kaydı paylaşılmayacak."
- Undecided replay, said plainly: "Yayın kaydının paylaşılmasına … güncel olarak karar verilecek."
- Capacity with its reason: "Ekibim en fazla 50 kişi ile ilgilenebilir. Çünkü; …"
- Double limit: "Ama yakında ya kontenjan ya da süre dolacak."
- Late-join window: "Eğer 24 saat içinde katılırsanız sorunsuz şekilde adapte olabilirsiniz."
- Price change with its reason and date: "[tarih] tarihinde [fiyat] yerine [fiyat]'a yükselecek."
- Soft: "Aynı fırsata ve davete sen de sahipsin. / Ama sonsuza kadar değil."
- Sunk effort (camp finale): "21 gün emek verdin. Şimdi final çizgisinde, hak ettiğin karşılığı almadan geri dönme."
Never extend, reopen or add "ek kontenjan" after a stated close; the archive shows every later deadline losing weight when that happens.

**How to vary a resend**
1. **Voice:** founder `ben` → team `biz` about "[Kurucu] Bey" → named team member or coach with personality → bare ops reminder. Three to five angles in 48 hours is the house ceiling; fewer is safer.
2. **Angle:** benefits → pain list → objections/SSS → gift → student story → registration status.
3. **Avatar:** same slot, one version per declared segment ("Bu programa katılırken bize tek bir şey söyledin: 'İşimi büyütmek istiyorum.'").
4. **Register of the same bullets:** literal → one extended metaphor → social proof.
5. **Tense, invite → replay → upsell:** keep the bullets; switch future to past ("anlatacak" → "anlattı"), switch the CTA to the panel, then add the offer for non-members.
6. **Back-reference:** "Bir önceki mailde vurguladığımız gibi, …" Only when that email really went out.
7. **Same body, alternative subject:** change the time token or name position (see `turkish-subject-lines.md`).

Length bands: "Başladık" 40 to 70 words; agenda 70 to 130; community push 40 to 90; belief and story 180 to 300; sales 150 to 350.

The member agenda mails drifted into an impersonal passive register for a few weeks ("ele alınacak", "işlenecektir") and returned to direct address with a pain-question opener; treat the passive phase as a deviation.

On sen/siz, `patron-notes.md` advises siz for cold lists and sen for buyers, while the newest launch in the archive writes sen to everyone. Choose by list temperature and the expert's voice, then keep it constant inside the email. On format, `email-machine.md` asks for a plain-text look; the house adds a linked banner at the bottom of agenda mails. Prefer text links, and at most one image.

## 6. Planning procedure: from a brief to a send calendar

**Step 1. Ask for what is missing.**
- Event type: free launch (1 to 3 evenings), 21-day cohort, member broadcast, short offer, onboarding mini-camp, bad-news notice.
- Date and time of each session; time zone note if readers are abroad.
- Audience segments and temperature: cold list, registrants, attendees, non-attendees, buyers, members, alumni; any declared avatar or goal.
- CTA target at each stage: registration page, community link, live link, replay/panel, appointment form, checkout.
- Real constraints: is there a replay; seat limit and its reason; price or bonus deadline; access end date. If none exists, plan without urgency mails.
- Voices available: founder, team, named coach or team member.
- Proof available: named student results, screenshots, numbers that can be shown.

**Step 2. Pick the architecture** from section 2 and combine where needed (a launch = a + e, then b for buyers; each cohort broadcast = c).

**Step 3. Lay the backbone.** Fix T for each session, then place: the reminder trio per session; replay at T+1 morning; for (a) the eve story at T-1 evening and community pushes at registration, T-2, T-1; for (b) the weekly live blocks and the seminar block from T-5.

**Step 4. Fill the slots with types** from section 3. For every slot record: send time, segment, sender voice, type, angle, CTA target, and which constraint (if any) it cites.

**Step 5. Apply the gates.**
- Exclude from invitations anyone who already registered; move them to reminders.
- Sales, quota and close mails go only to people who saw the offer (attended, watched the replay, or clicked the offer page). Buyers leave immediately and enter orientation.
- Community pushes stop for people who joined.
- Cap the day: the house often sends 3 to 5 mails on event days; plan at most the reminder trio plus one persuasive mail per segment unless the user asks for more.
- After the last mail, the segment returns to weekly value emails (`email-machine.md` section 7).

**Step 6. Vary the resends** with section 4: no two consecutive mails to one segment share both voice and angle.

**Step 7. Check before delivery.**
- One register (sen or siz) per email; `{{isim}}` has a fallback.
- Each urgency line names a constraint that is true; nothing reopens after the close.
- No income promises, no statistics without a source, no subjects that imitate system alerts or personal messages.
- Dates, weekdays and times match in subject, body and CTA; group and live links are tested.
- Each email has one job and one destination.

**Output:** a table (#, date/time, segment, voice, type, subject idea, angle, CTA target, constraint cited), then the emails in order.
